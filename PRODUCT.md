# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Overseas importers and brand buyers (Japan today; Europe, the US and Australia next) evaluating Cosmo Textile as a manufacturing partner for home décor, table linen, kitchen textiles and fabric accessories. They arrive from a referral, a trade contact or a search, skim on desktop or phone, and decide whether to send an enquiry.

## Product Purpose
A marketing site for a Karur, India manufacturer-exporter. It establishes credibility (decades of export practice to Japanese standards, in-house supply chain, quality and compliance) and turns interest into enquiries through the contact form. Success is a qualified enquiry with product list, target price and market.

## Brand Personality
Trusted, crafted, calm. The quiet confidence of a long-standing exporter: real photographs of real products and facilities, measured motion, no hype. Let the textiles and the craft speak.

## Anti-references
- Generic B2B trade-portal catalogue (IndiaMART / Alibaba style): stock photos, dense product grids, banner clutter.
- Flashy startup / SaaS styling: gradients, glowing buttons, big stat counters, hype copy.

## Design Principles
1. Show the real thing: own photography of products, workshop and showroom over stock or illustration.
2. Calm confidence: motion and type are measured; one considered moment beats many effects.
3. Buyer's questions first: what they make, to what standard, at what scale, and how to start.
4. Craft in the details: textile-inspired touches (unrolling, weave, stitch) used sparingly, never as costume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA (assumed, not yet confirmed with the owner). All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; content must never depend on an animation finishing to be visible.
