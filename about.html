A textile house built to an international standard from day one.

Our story

Cosmo Textile was established in April 2021 as a manufacturer and exporter of high-quality home furnishings, fabrics and made-ups. From the start, the goal was simple: supply a wide range of quality products at competitive prices, deliver them on time, and work relentlessly for the satisfaction of every customer.

We created a production environment designed to meet international standards, and we build long-term global relationships rather than one-off orders. Visitors are always welcome to see the workshop for themselves.

Made in Karur

A home-textile town, and a craft tradition

Karur in Tamil Nadu is one of India's established clusters for home-textile export, with a deep local base of weaving, dyeing, printing and stitching skill. We draw on that ecosystem for capacity, and keep the parts that most affect quality, stitching and inspection, inside our own factory.
