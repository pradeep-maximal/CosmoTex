Yarn with a second life.

We turn plastic waste into textiles that still feel and look the part. Two routes: yarn spun from recycled PET bottles, and fabric woven from post-consumer recycled yarns.

Recycled PET-bottle yarn

Yarn derived from 100% recycled PET bottles. The process converts plastic waste into strong, lightweight fibres, well suited to apparel, upholstery and outdoor textiles. It supports a circular economy and keeps bottles out of landfill.

Post-consumer recycled yarn

Fabrics made from post-consumer recycled yarns, available in cotton blends, mélange and fancy-yarn options. They keep durability, softness and vibrant colour, and suit fashion, home textiles and eco-conscious brands.

The idea, in one number

60

half-litre PET bottles is roughly what one kilogram of our recycled yarn keeps out of landfill.

A closed loop, by design

Bottles are collected, cleaned, chipped and spun back into fibre. That fibre is knitted or woven, finished, and stitched into the same home and lifestyle products we already make, then shipped to buyers who want a lower-impact line without a trade-off in quality.
