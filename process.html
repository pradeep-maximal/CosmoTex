Home › Process

From yarn to carton, in six coordinated steps.

We buy yarn directly, run dyeing and weaving on dedicated contract capacity, and keep stitching, inspection and packing under our own roof.

1

Yarn

Bought straight from the mill

We purchase high-quality yarns directly from mills, so we control fibre, count and quality from the very first input.

2

Dyeing

Six dedicated dyeing units

Six cabinet dyeing units work for us on a contract basis, giving repeatable shade matching across large runs.

3

Weaving

Woven to our constructions

Contract auto looms weave plain grounds, dobby and jacquard designs, stripes and checks to our specifications.

4

Stitching

Twenty Juki machines, in-house

Stitching runs in our own factory, so make, fit and finish stay under our direct control.

5

Inspection

Fifty-plus checkers

Staff inspect the finished product through trimming, first check and final check before it is passed.

6

Packing

Packed, then needle-checked

Packed to each customer's requirement, with metal and needle checking on every piece before it goes in the carton.

The last gate

Nothing ships without needle checking

Every finished piece passes through a Hashima 790G metal and needle detector in a dedicated room. It is the standard Japanese buyers expect, and it travels to every other market we serve.
